## SOLUTION: What is PromptLingo? (1 minute)

PromptLingo is a **professional voice amplifier** powered by AI. It doesn't teach you English—it instantly transforms your casual, informal, or poorly-expressed input into flawless, context-aware, professional output in your target language.

Here's how it works:

**1. Speak or Type Naturally**
- You can record your voice in Spanish, Haitian Creole, English—whatever feels comfortable

**3. Instant Professional Transformation**
- In **5 seconds**, PromptLingo converts your input into native-speaker-quality output

## UNIQUE FEATURES (1 minute, 15 seconds)

What makes PromptLingo different from every other translation tool?

**Feature #1: The Competence Meter**
This is our signature feature. After every translation, you see a visual **before-and-after comparison** scoring your text across five metrics:
- Grammar correctness
- Tone appropriateness
- Vocabulary sophistication
- Domain-specific terminology (e.g., medical terms)
- Overall professional score (0-100%)

It shows the user, "Your casual input scored 42%—your professional output scored 89%. **Your competence is now visible.**" That feedback is emotionally transformative. It validates what they already knew: they're intelligent; they just needed the right words.

**Feature #2: Continuous Recording with Speaker Identification**
This is something Google Translate doesn't have. PromptLingo can record entire conversations with **multiple speakers** and automatically:
- Identify different speakers
- Color-code them (Speaker 1 is blue, Speaker 2 is green)
- Generate a timestamped transcript with speaker labels

**Use case:** A doctor-patient interview where the patient speaks Spanish and needs an English transcript for medical records. PromptLingo captures both voices, translates the conversation, and exports a professional document.

**Feature #3: Privacy-First Design**
We know Sofia is translating sensitive medical information. That's why:
- Translation history is **encrypted and stored locally** on your device, never on our servers
- History auto-deletes after 30 days
- No tracking, no selling your data

**Feature #4: Cultural Intelligence**
We don't just translate words—we understand cultural context:
- For Spanish: appropriate use of "tú" vs. "usted" based on formality
- For Haitian Creole: standardized orthography (not French-based spelling)
- For all languages: proper greetings, formal address, and domain-specific phrasing

## PRESENTATION DELIVERY TIPS

**Pace:** Aim for 120-140 words per minute (conversational, not rushed)

**Energy:** Start strong with Sofia's story—make it emotionally relatable. Speak with conviction when describing social impact.

**Visuals:** If you have a screen, show:
- Side-by-side before/after translation comparison
- The Competence Meter in action (visual scores)
- A quick demo (5-second transformation)
- Pricing table (emphasize $9.99 tier)

**Body Language:**
- Make eye contact with audience members
- Use hand gestures when emphasizing key points ("5 seconds," "2-3 hours saved")
- Speak with empathy when describing the target user's challenges

**Tone Shifts:**
- **Empathetic** when describing the target user's challenges
- **Confident** when explaining the solution
- **Urgent** when discussing social impact
- **Welcoming** when giving the call to action

**Practice:** Rehearse aloud 3-4 times to get comfortable with the flow. Time yourself—aim for 4:30-5:00 minutes to leave room for natural pauses and emphasis.
